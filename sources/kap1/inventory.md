# Inventory: kap1 (Matematik X, kapitel 1 — tal i decimalform, bråk, avrundning)

Input for World 1. The photos are used to design tasks. They are not copied into the game; tasks get fresh numbers.
Task types are proposals (all **NEW**: no generators exist yet). Final names are decided in the spec.

## Sheets → levels (suggested order: easiest first)

### 1. `decimal-numbers.png` — "Räkna med tal i decimalform"
- **Skills:**
  - add and subtract decimals
  - multiply and divide by 10 / 100 / 1 000
  - decimal · whole number (incl. tens and hundreds)
  - decimal · decimal
  - decimal / small whole number
- **Task types:**
  - `decimal-add-sub`
  - `scale-by-power-of-ten` (· and / with 10, 100, 1 000)
  - `decimal-multiply`
  - `decimal-divide-by-integer`
- **Examples:** `0,7 + 0,5`, `4 − 0,3`, `100 · 0,76`, `45,3/10`, `0,3 · 60`, `0,7 · 0,5`, `2,5/5`,
  `100,1 − 99,9`, `19 / 1 000`
- **Range:** one to three decimal places. Divisions come out exact.

### 2. `multiply-decimals.png` — "Multiplikation med tal i decimalform"
- **Skills:**
  - whole number · decimal
  - paired a/b tasks that show the pattern (`7 · 0,1` vs `7 · 0,01`; `0,3 · 20` vs `0,3 · 200`)
- **Task types:** `decimal-multiply`, with a "pair" mode that shows the a and b tasks together.
- **Examples:** `6 · 0,04`, `25 · 0,01`, `8 · 0,06`, `40 · 0,02`, `500 · 0,9`
- **Range:** whole numbers 2–500; decimals with one or two decimal places.
- **Note:** this needs the multiplication tables. It is the best place for the multiplication helper camp.

### 3. `mix.png` — "Blandad form och bråkform"
- **Skill:** convert a mixed number to an improper fraction, using pictures of pies cut into parts.
- **Task type:** `mixed-to-improper` with a visual. Tap the pieces to colour them, then type the numerator.
- **Examples:** `1 ¾ = 7/4`, `2 ⅔ = ?/3`, `1 ⅗ = ?/5`, `4 ½ = ?/2`, `2 = ?/4`, `3 ⅓ = ?/3`
- **Range:** whole part 1–4; denominators 2, 3, 4, 5.

### 4. `how-do-you-count-1.png` — "Hur räknar du? 1"
- **Skill:** choose the right *calculation* for a word problem, without calculating it.
- **Task type:** `choose-expression`: multiple choice A/B/C.
- **Typical problems:**
  - share equally (/)
  - per month → per year (·12)
  - unit conversion (ton → kg)
  - consumption per mil
  - people per km²
  - "hur många gånger fler"
  - chained conversion (7 · 24 · 60)
  - proportion (bread/flour)
- **Wrong options come from typical mistakes:**
  - inverted division
  - wrong operation
  - wrong count (5 vs 4 friends)
  - unit not converted

### 5. `how-do-you-count-2.png` — "Hur räknar du? 2"
- **Skill:** same as sheet 4, with decimal quantities.
- **Task type:** `choose-expression`.
- **Typical problems:**
  - price per kg
  - difference between decimals
  - "0,2 m längre än" (subtract)
  - reverse a division
  - unit price from a bag
  - hundredths of a second
  - remove the container weight then divide (hg → kg)
  - liters ÷ dl portions

### 6. `repeat-chap-1-1.png` — "Vi repeterar Kap 1 ver 1" (review)
- **Skills:**
  - number words → digits ("sjutusen sextiofem", "sju tusendelar")
  - place value of a digit
  - largest and smallest of four numbers (incl. negatives)
  - compare two numbers (decimals, unit fractions)
  - fraction + decimal
  - round to heltal and tiotal
  - mental arithmetic (+ − · /)
  - estimation (överslagsräkning)
- **Task types:**
  - `words-to-number`
  - `place-value`
  - `order-numbers`
  - `compare-numbers`
  - `fraction-decimal-add-sub`
  - `round`
  - `mental-arithmetic`
  - `estimate`

### 7. `repeat-chap-1-2.png` — "Vi repeterar Kap 1 ver 2" (review)
- **Skills:**
  - largest and smallest of decimals
  - negative-number arithmetic (`−7 − 2`, `3 − 8 − 2`)
  - fractions with the same denominator, incl. mixed numbers (`2 3/7 − 1 5/7`)
  - decimal + − ·
  - scale by 10/100/1 000
  - big-number division (`6 000/200`, `20/0,5`)
  - round to hundredths
  - read a number line (0–1,1, step 0,01)
  - choose the best estimate
- **Task types:**
  - `order-numbers`
  - `negative-arithmetic`
  - `fraction-same-denominator`
  - `decimal-add-sub`
  - `decimal-multiply`
  - `scale-by-power-of-ten`
  - `divide`
  - `round`
  - `number-line`
  - `estimate` (multiple choice)

## Suggested world layout
- **7 banor**, ordered as above. Sheets 6 and 7 are review mixes, so they work well as the last two
  banor before the boss.
- **Hjälpläger (helper camps):**
  - Multiplikation, incl. tables and "flytta decimalkommat"
  - Division, incl. dividing decimals and division as a fraction
  - Addition & subtraktion med decimaltal (line the decimal commas up)
  - Bråk: blandad form ↔ bråkform, with pie pictures
  - Optional: Negativa tal (number line)
- **Slutboss:** about 8 tasks mixing one task from each bana. Defeating it shows a summary of every
  bana cleared and the skills practised.
